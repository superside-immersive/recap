Innovation friction

Operational Friction and Innovation Leakage

Two things are limiting growth: ambition is being narrowed too early, and internal AI exploration is not being consolidated into reusable knowledge.

Internal marketing narrows the work.

Requests for simple AR or safe executions make the offer smaller than the capability. Enterprise needs to see unique, high-scale, emotionally resonant experiences.

AI research disappears in Slack.

Explorations become isolated threads. Without a central area, work gets duplicated months later instead of becoming products, playbooks, or demos.

Missing capabilities

Critical Capability Gaps

Experiential work depends on feeling, timing, interaction, and live context. Static 3D craft matters, but it cannot carry the full experience alone.

UX / Motion is critical.

We need profiles that bring humanity, autonomy, behavioral flow, and complex motion into deliverables. This is the difference between a visual asset and an experience.

Fast-turnaround editing.

Events create PR value in hours, not weeks. A dedicated editor can iterate same-day videos and capitalize on the moment while it is still alive.

Capability-fit note: profiles optimized for static or graphic 3D are not always the right fit to lead emotional, interactive, or client-facing experiential concepts. The operating model should make that distinction explicit.

Proposed leadership

Head of Creative Technology Level 5a

For this area to function and innovate, it needs clear ownership from someone who can direct concept, technology, client communication, and hands-on development without separating strategy from execution.

Why the level matters

Level 5a Unlocks Execution

Holistic experiential projects already require cross-functional leadership across concept, technology, production, and communication. The current gap is authority, not responsibility.

Formal authority removes unnecessary friction.

Without the role level to match the work, leadership has to be forced across teams and seniority lines. Level 5a makes the structure legible and lets the work move faster.

Cross-functional alignment

Clear ownership for how resources from other areas should contribute.

Hands-on strategy

Business design and team management without losing proximity to the creative and technical work.
